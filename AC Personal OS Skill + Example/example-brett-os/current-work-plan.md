# Current Work Plan

_Current as of 2026-06-16. Refresh the 90-day section each quarter._

## Sweet spot
Demand generation for a mid-market B2B SaaS product with a considered, multi-stakeholder buying cycle. We are best at webinar-and-content-led campaigns that warm up a named account list, then hand qualified opportunities to sales. We are not good at, and should stop chasing, top-of-funnel brand plays with no pipeline tie, and pure self-serve PLG motions. That is not our buyer.

## Non-negotiables
- We do not buy lists or send unsolicited cold blasts. Inbound and named-account outbound only.
- We do not promise features in campaigns that the product cannot deliver this quarter.
- Sales and marketing share one definition of a qualified opportunity. We do not move the line to hit a number.

## Where it is going
- **Long term:** marketing sources 50 percent of net-new pipeline (today it is about 32 percent).
- **This year:** hit $14M in marketing-sourced pipeline, launch the new "Supplier Risk" module campaign, and get the lead-to-opportunity handoff under 48 hours.
- **Must be true by year end:** webinar program running monthly without heroics, attribution we trust, and one repeatable campaign playbook the team can run without me in the room.

## Pulse metrics (weekly)
Marketing-sourced pipeline ($), MQL-to-opportunity conversion (%), webinar registrants and attendance rate, opportunities created, average lead-to-opportunity time (hours), content pieces published, paid spend vs. pipeline (ratio).

## Open bets (unsettled, not strategy)
Testing an AI-assisted content engine to triple published output without adding headcount. Promising but unproven on quality and on whether it actually moves pipeline. Do not treat as committed strategy yet.

## Who we serve / who we turn away
Ideal: VP Supply Chain or Director of Procurement at a 500 to 5,000 employee manufacturer or distributor. Not a fit: small businesses under 100 employees, pure software companies with no physical supply chain, and anyone shopping on price alone.
