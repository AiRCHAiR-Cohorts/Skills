# Tools and Systems

## Daily stack
- **HubSpot**: marketing automation, email, landing pages, campaign tracking
- **Salesforce**: CRM and the source of truth for pipeline and opportunities
- **Google Workspace**: email, calendar, docs, sheets
- **Slack**: team and cross-functional communication
- **Asana**: campaign and project management
- **Zoom Webinars**: webinar delivery
- **Looker**: dashboards and attribution reporting

## How it is set up
HubSpot and Salesforce are synced, with lead routing rules in Salesforce (the SLA fix project lives here). Pipeline reporting flows Salesforce to Looker. Campaign UTMs follow a fixed naming convention, do not invent new ones. The weekly pulse metrics live in a Looker dashboard called "DG Weekly."

## Where my data lives
- Pipeline and opportunity data: Salesforce and Looker
- Campaign performance: HubSpot
- Plans, briefs, reports: Google Drive, in the "Demand Gen" shared drive
- Project status: Asana

## Evaluating or adopting
Piloting an AI writing workflow for the content engine. Looking at a webinar platform upgrade for better engagement data.

## Tried and stopped
Dropped a standalone ABM platform last year. It duplicated what HubSpot plus Salesforce already did and the team would not adopt it. Lesson: do not add a tool that overlaps the core stack unless it clearly replaces something.
