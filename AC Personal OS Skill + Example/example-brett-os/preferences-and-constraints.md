# Preferences and Constraints

## Hard constraints
- Central Time. Core hours 8:30am to 5:30pm.
- No meetings before 9am, I block that hour for focused work.
- Fridays after 2pm are for planning, not new meetings.
- I do not work weekends except in a launch crunch, and even then I decide, do not assume.

## Strong preferences
- Lead with the answer, then support it. Never bury the recommendation.
- Anything customer-facing goes through me before it ships.
- Tie every campaign idea to a pipeline impact, or do not bring it.
- Use the existing UTM and naming conventions, do not invent new ones.

## Things I hate
- Meetings that should have been a Slack message.
- Status updates with no number in them.
- Being asked "for a quick report" that is actually a half-day build.
- Drafts that sound like a press release.

## Personal constraints
- School pickup Tuesdays and Thursdays at 3:30pm, hard stop, no meetings then.

## How I want AI output
Short. Lead with the recommendation or the answer. Use bullets for options, prose for reasoning. Give me a draft I can send, not an outline I have to finish. Always show the number or the source when you make a claim. No em dashes, no hype words. If you are unsure, say so and ask, do not guess and dress it up.
