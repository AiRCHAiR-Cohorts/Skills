# Team and Relationships

## Dana Reyes: CMO (my manager)
Weekly 1:1 plus the monthly business review. Dana needs a clear pipeline read and early warning on risks, no surprises. I need air cover with the CEO and fast decisions on budget. She is data-first and impatient with fluffy updates. Lead with the number, then the story. Working Genius: high Wonder and Discernment, so she will probe the "why" and the "should we" before the "how."

## Priya Shah: Demand Gen Manager (direct report)
Twice-weekly check-ins, otherwise async. Priya needs clear priorities and room to run. I need reliable execution and a heads-up when a date is going to slip. She is my strongest operator. Do not micromanage her, give her the outcome and the deadline.

## Sofia Mendez: Content Lead (direct report)
Weekly 1:1. Sofia needs creative direction and protection from last-minute scope changes. I need quality drafts on time and an honest read on the AI content pilot. She is skeptical of AI writing quality, so keep her in the loop and respect her standards.

## Marcus Lee: Marketing Ops (direct report)
Weekly 1:1, lots of async. Marcus needs precise requirements and patience with technical constraints. I need clean data and the SLA fix delivered. He gets frustrated when asked for "a quick report" that is not actually quick. Be specific about what I need and why.

## Tom Becker: VP Sales (peer, critical partner)
Weekly pipeline sync. Tom needs qualified opportunities and a shared definition of "qualified." I need his reps to work the leads we send and give feedback. Relationship is good but tense around lead quality. Never blindside him in a group setting, raise issues with him one-on-one first.

## Media agency (Catalyst): vendor
Biweekly call. They need clear targets and timely approvals. I need performance and proactive recommendations, not just reporting. Hold them to the named-account strategy.
