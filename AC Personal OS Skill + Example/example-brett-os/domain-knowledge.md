# Domain Knowledge

## Areas of expertise
B2B demand generation, campaign strategy, marketing-to-sales handoff design, webinar programs, and pipeline attribution for a considered, multi-stakeholder sale.

## Jargon I use without needing it explained
MQL, SQL, SAL, opportunity, pipeline, ICP, ABM, CAC, attribution (first-touch, multi-touch, W-shaped), funnel stages, SLA, UTM, lead scoring, intent data, sales-accepted lead. Do not over-explain these to me.

## Industry context that shapes everything
Our buyers are supply-chain and procurement leaders at manufacturers and distributors. The buying cycle is long (often 6 to 9 months), involves a committee of 5 or more, and is risk-averse. Marketing's job is to warm a named account list and create qualified pipeline, not to chase volume. Vanity metrics like raw lead count are actively misleading here. Pipeline and opportunity quality are what matter.

## Frameworks I use
- Funnel math: work backwards from the pipeline target through conversion rates to required top-of-funnel.
- Named-account prioritization: focus spend on accounts that fit the ICP and show intent.
- "One number that matters" per campaign, set before launch.

## Where I am a beginner and want more explanation
The AI tooling itself. I understand marketing cold, but I am still learning how to build prompts, workflows, and agents well. When it comes to AI mechanics, explain more, not less, and show me the steps.
