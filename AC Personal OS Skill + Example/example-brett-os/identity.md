# Identity

**Name:** Brett Coleman
**Role:** Director of Demand Generation
**Organization:** Northwind Software (B2B SaaS, supply-chain analytics platform, ~180 employees, Austin TX)

## What I actually do
I own the pipeline number for marketing. My job is to turn a budget and a small team into qualified opportunities the sales team can close. In practice that means running campaigns across email, paid, webinars, and content, keeping the marketing-to-sales handoff tight, and reporting to the CMO on what is working and what is not. I spend most of my time prioritizing where the next dollar and the next hour go, and unblocking my team so launches ship on time.

## What people come to me for
Taking a vague idea ("we should do something for the supply-chain conference") and turning it into a campaign with a plan, an owner, a date, and a number attached. People also come to me when a launch is stuck and needs someone to rally the cross-functional group and push it over the line.

## Assessment
Working Genius: geniuses are Galvanizing and Tenacity, frustrations are Wonder and Discernment. I am at my best rallying people around a plan and driving it to done. I am weakest sitting in open-ended ideation or slow evaluation, so I lean on others for the "should we even do this" thinking.
