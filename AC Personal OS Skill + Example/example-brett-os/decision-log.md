# Decision Log

## How I decide
Fast and pragmatic, biased toward action. I gather the few numbers that matter, get a read from the one or two people closest to it, then commit. I do not wait for perfect information. Working Genius says I am low on Wonder and Discernment, so I deliberately pull in someone who will pressure-test the "should we" before I lock in, otherwise I move too fast.

## What I want before deciding
The number it affects, the cost, who owns it, and one informed opinion from someone who disagrees with me. That is usually enough.

## Recent decisions

**Cut the standalone ABM platform (last year).**
The tool overlapped HubSpot and Salesforce, cost $40k a year, and the team would not adopt it. I gathered usage data (under 20 percent), confirmed the overlap with Marcus, and killed it despite having championed it originally. Lesson logged: do not add tools that overlap the core stack.

**Moved webinars to monthly cadence (this quarter).**
We were running webinars ad hoc and they drove our best pipeline, but inconsistently. Rather than keep treating each as a one-off, I committed to a monthly program and gave it to Priya to own. The bet: consistency beats occasional brilliance. Still proving out.

## How I handle uncertainty
I make the call reversible if I can. Pilot small, set a clear read-out date and a kill criterion, then decide based on what happens. The AI content engine is being run exactly this way: pilot, 90-day read, kill if quality or pipeline does not hold up.
