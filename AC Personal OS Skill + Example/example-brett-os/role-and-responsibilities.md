# Role and Responsibilities

## Core accountabilities
- Marketing-sourced pipeline number (the one that lands on me if it misses)
- Demand gen budget and how it is allocated across channels
- Campaign calendar and on-time launches
- The marketing-to-sales handoff and SLA
- Coaching and unblocking a team of five

## A typical week
- **Monday:** team standup, review last week's pulse metrics, set the week's priorities.
- **Tuesday:** 1:1s with direct reports, campaign working sessions.
- **Wednesday:** cross-functional launch syncs (product, sales, design), webinar dry runs.
- **Thursday:** pipeline and attribution review, agency call, build or review campaign assets.
- **Friday:** report to CMO, plan next week, clear the backlog.

## Routine decisions I make weekly
- Which campaign or asset ships first when the team is over capacity
- Where to shift paid budget based on what is converting
- Whether a lead batch is qualified enough to route to sales
- Approve or send back copy and creative

## What I produce
Campaign briefs, the monthly campaign calendar, the weekly pipeline report for the CMO, webinar run-of-show docs, and quarterly budget reallocation proposals.

## Reporting
Report to: Dana Reyes, CMO.
Direct reports: Priya (demand gen manager), Sofia (content lead), Marcus (marketing ops), plus two campaign specialists.

## Cadences that shape my work
Monthly webinar cycle, monthly CMO business review, quarterly planning and budget reset, and the annual marketing kickoff in January.
